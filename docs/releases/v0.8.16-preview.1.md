# KMReader 0.8.16 preview

EPUB curl snapshots preserve empty chapter anchors instead of introducing hidden
line boxes that change margin collapse and displace subsequent columns. The
visible text now retains its words, spacing and pagination during the fold.
Preformatted notes soft-wrap within their column, preserving their text and
explicit line breaks without overflowing onto adjacent pages.

Adjacent chapter boundary pages are prepared in an isolated temporary view.
Dragging across a chapter boundary can show the curl before pointer release,
without replacing the live input iframe or committing the reading position.
Only one temporary chapter is rendered at a time; its DOM and observers are
released after capture. Prepared images are bounded and cleared on reader close.
Idle preparation prioritizes the current/forward textures and has a scheduling
deadline so continuous activity cannot indefinitely postpone warming.

Large initial gesture displacement catches up over at most 90 ms; small input
and subsequent tracking remain direct. Releasing during entry continues from the
displayed position. Input received during preparation cannot draw an unready
surface. Compositor capture waits for delivery only while preceding layout or
surface changes still need retirement; that barrier observes real browser frames.

Regression checks cover live/full/compact text pixels, empty chapter markers,
long preformatted notes, single/double columns, RTL, held drags over both chapter
boundaries, retained input/CFI, prepared texture reuse, early release, interrupted
input and rapid changes among all transition styles. Native Android WebView and
Windows WebView2 checks exercise the shipped reader and recovery paths.
