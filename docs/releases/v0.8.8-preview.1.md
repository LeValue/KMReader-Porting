# KMReader Port 0.8.8 development preview

Curl shadows follow the projected sheet geometry through a reusable,
depth-tested shadow mask and separable GPU blur. Curved free edges no longer
use disconnected straight shadow segments. Active EPUB folds have no fixed
10-second cancellation deadline; release, pointer cancellation, resize, modal
suspension and closing still clean up the gesture.

Reader controls derive their surface, text and button colors from the selected
page palette. Explicit local button themes override incompatible application
icon colors, with a contrast fallback for gray, sepia, light and dark pages.
Header/footer reading labels use the EPUB text palette as well.

Centered comic sheets leave through the outer margin as the turn finishes;
spread backs settle onto the incoming leaf with matching position and lighting.
Removing the final overlay no longer abruptly removes a visible folded page.
Double-page comics share a height and meet at the spine, with letterboxing
outside the complete spread. Live pages, prepared frames and curl bounds use
the same geometry, including unequal page ratios and RTL reading.

EPUB typography previews update with the settings panel still open. Slider
updates are coalesced over 80ms, with at most one browser layout running and
one latest pending value; releasing a slider or selecting an option flushes
the final value. Lazy settings rows and the retained browser surface remain.

Install the APK for your architecture (usually ARM64), or extract the complete
Windows ZIP. Build 18 retains preview package/certificate update support.
Local formats remain EPUB, PDF and CBZ/ZIP. Physical frame rates vary by device.
