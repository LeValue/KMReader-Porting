# KMReader Port 0.8.6 development preview

Canceled EPUB slide/curl gestures retain the original snapshot until the live
publication is restored, avoiding a wrong-page flash. Same-document cancellation
restores the existing scrollport without reopening and restabilizing the chapter.
A fixed-layout native view that ignores its first turn is detected from its
unchanged viewport; the adjacent spine item is displayed after a delivery
check, without consuming another user tap.
Pointer-driven GPU updates are coalesced to one draw per animation frame;
texture and GPU-program preparation run at idle and prepares only the next reading position.

Backward curl peels the opposite leaf instead of replaying the forward fold
backward. Curvature is concentrated near the fold by default, with a persisted
**Curl deformation reach / Estensione della curvatura** option in both readers
and their global defaults. The tighter, more flexible section has continuous
position/tangent joins, front lighting and depth-linked shadows under both the roll and free edges.
Gestures use the width of the turning leaf/artwork. EPUB header/footer labels
float over the continuous reading surface, removing the separate clipping bar.

Comic slide/cover retain the decoded image subtree at animation endpoints.
Comic curl uses a separate, fixed-layout presentation, crops its mesh to the
actual artwork (including either leaf in a spread), and waits for target artwork
before releasing the preview. Black letterbox margins stay outside the paper.
Native checks additionally rasterize comic folds in both physical directions.

Install the APK for your architecture (usually ARM64), or extract the complete
Windows ZIP. Build number 16 retains preview package/certificate update support.
This remains a development preview; controlled checks do not establish physical
frame rates on every device or exact equivalence with Apple's renderer.
