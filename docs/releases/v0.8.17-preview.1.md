# KMReader 0.8.17 preview

Comic curl keeps the stationary page, letterbox and moving front opaque through
the entire turn. The old last-28% stationary crossfade and last-4% front fade are
removed. The projected free edge reveals the replacement geometrically, including
unequal fitted page sizes, while retaining the current page's original scale.
Subpixel endpoint geometry retires at the exact destination instead of leaving
one mesh triangle of old ink or using a timed transparency transition.

Shared host/Android/Windows pixel probes cover opaque stationary paper in the
last quarter of a double-page curl, both physical directions, moving paper faces,
mixed dimensions and unchanged final page pixels. EPUB rendering is unchanged.

A separate Kindle feasibility report documents current Amazon EPUB/PDF exports,
account/library/download requirements, the two proposed projects, format conversion,
and the limits of reading protected Kindle content with KMReader's own engine.
This build does not add an Amazon account connection or claim Kindle compatibility.
